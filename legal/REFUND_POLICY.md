# Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Crechely is a trading name of Straight Glow. This policy explains how
subscriptions, cancellations and refunds work for schools using Crechely.
Questions: **support@crechely.co.za**.

## 1. Free trial

- Every new school gets a **30-day free trial** with full access.
- No card is needed to start, and **nothing is charged automatically** when
  the trial ends. You only pay if you choose a plan on the Billing page.
- If you don't subscribe, your school becomes read-only: everything stays
  visible and can be exported, but nothing new can be added until you
  subscribe.

## 2. Plans and prices

All prices are in South African rand (ZAR) and are charged through Paystack.

- **Monthly:** R499 a month.
- **Yearly:** R4,990 a year, billed once a year.
- **Founding school (first 10 schools only):** R299 a month.

- One plan includes everything: no per-child fees and no set-up fees.
- The founding-school price stays at R299 a month for as long as the school
  stays subscribed. If a founding school cancels, the founding price ends.
- We give at least **30 days' notice** by email before any price change
  applies to your next renewal.

## 3. Renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period (monthly
or yearly) until you cancel. Paystack emails a receipt for every payment,
and every payment and receipt is also listed under **Settings → Billing**.

## 4. Cancelling

- An admin can cancel at any time under **Settings → Billing → Cancel
  subscription**, or by emailing support@crechely.co.za.
- Cancelling stops all future charges straight away.
- You keep full access until the end of the period you have already paid
  for. After that the school becomes read-only, as above.
- Your data is not deleted when you cancel. You can download a full backup
  of your school's data at any time under **Settings → Backup & export**,
  and resubscribe later to carry on where you left off.
- To remove your school's data completely, an admin can delete the school.
  It stays in the trash for 30 days (so it can be restored if that was a
  mistake) and is then permanently deleted.

## 5. Refunds

- **Yearly plan — 30-day money-back guarantee.** If Crechely isn't right for
  you, email us within 30 days of paying for a yearly plan and we refund it
  in full.
- **Monthly and founding plans.** Payments are not refunded for part of a
  month; cancelling stops the next payment and you keep access until the
  end of the month you paid for.
- **Charged by mistake?** If you were charged after cancelling, charged
  twice, or charged the wrong amount, we refund the incorrect charge in full.
- Refunds go back to the card or account you paid with, through Paystack.
  Once we approve a refund it usually reaches you within 5–10 business days,
  depending on your bank.

## 6. How to ask for a refund

Email **support@crechely.co.za** with your school's name and the receipt
number (on the receipt, or under Settings → Billing). We reply within 2
business days.
